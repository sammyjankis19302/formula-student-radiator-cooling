# Radiator CAD

SolidWorks part files of the radiator, saved on 9 August 2025, with their original file names. They were modelled from measurements of the radiator on the car (see `../original/sketch-2025-07-23.jpg`): 25 single-pass tubes at 10.6 mm pitch, a 256 mm core between the top and bottom tanks, 310 mm overall width, 40 mm depth, inlet and outlet stubs and two mounting tabs.

|---|---|

| `radiator_without_flow_parameters_showcase_23.SLDPRT` | 21.2 MB |
| `rough.SLDPRT` | 20.3 MB |

They open in SolidWorks. The free eDrawings viewer can display them without a SolidWorks licence. Front and isometric views are in `../original/cad-front.jpg` and `../original/cad-isometric.jpg`.
